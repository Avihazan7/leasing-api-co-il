# Marketing Strategy Framework

**Module:** `marketing-strategy-framework.md`
**Version:** 1.0.0
**Author:** Avraham Bar Yochai Chazan — Claude Operating System
**Status:** Active — Business layer. Loaded on demand for marketing strategy tasks.
**Integrates with:** `OPERATING_SYSTEM.md`, `COMMAND_API.md`, `MEMORY.md`

---

A 10-prompt framework for building a complete marketing strategy from psychology to a one-page plan. Fill in the bracketed placeholders and run each prompt in order — outputs from earlier prompts feed the later ones.

---

## 1. The Customer Psychology Deep-Dive

> My business is [describe] and I sell [product/service] to [audience]. Don't give me demographics. Go deeper. What is my customer secretly afraid of? What do they tell their friends vs. what they actually feel? What have they tried before and why did it disappoint them? What does success look like in their mind — and what's stopping them from believing they can have it? Turn this into a psychological profile I can use to write marketing that feels like I'm reading their mind.

---

## 2. The Positioning Weapon

> Here are 3 of my biggest competitors: [list them]. Analyze how each one positions itself — their messaging, their promises, their tone. Now find the gap none of them are owning. Build me a positioning strategy that puts my business [describe] in a category of its own. Give me the exact language, the angle, and why customers will choose me without comparing prices.

---

## 3. The Message-Market Fit Test

> Here is my current marketing message: [paste it]. Here is my ideal customer profile: [paste it]. Be brutally honest. Does this message speak directly to this customer's pain, desire, and identity — or does it sound like every other business in my space? Score it 1–10 and rewrite it so it scores a 9 or higher. Explain every change you made and why.

---

## 4. The Viral Content Framework

> My business is [describe] targeting [ideal customer]. Generate 20 content ideas that would stop my customer mid-scroll. Each idea must trigger one of these emotions: curiosity, fear of missing out, relief, or aspiration. Label the emotion for each idea. Then pick the top 3 and write the full hook line for each — the first sentence that makes them impossible to skip.

---

## 5. The Full-Funnel Builder

> I run [business] selling [product] at [price] to [audience]. Map my complete customer journey from the moment they've never heard of me to the moment they buy and tell a friend. For every stage — Awareness, Interest, Consideration, Decision, Retention — give me: the customer's mindset, the right marketing channel, the content format that works best, and the exact message I should deliver. Make it a system I can hand to a team.

---

## 6. The Objection Annihilator

> My product/service is [describe] priced at [price]. List every reason a qualified customer would hesitate to buy — including the ones they'd never say out loud. Then give me a marketing response for each objection: one for my website, one for my ads, and one for my sales conversations. Make the responses feel human, not salesy.

---

## 7. The Email Sequence That Sells

> Build me a 7-email nurture sequence for [business] targeting [customer]. Email 1 must build trust with zero selling. Emails 2–5 must educate, handle objections, and build desire using storytelling and social proof. Email 6 makes the offer. Email 7 follows up with urgency. Write the subject line, preview text, and full body for every email. Every email must end with one clear next step.

---

## 8. The Paid Ads Architect

> I want to run paid ads for [business] with a budget of [$X/month] targeting [audience] on [platform]. Design my complete ad strategy. Include: campaign structure for cold vs. warm audiences, 3 different ad angles based on my customer's pain points, the exact copy and CTA for each angle, how to structure my retargeting, and what metrics tell me an ad is working vs. wasting money. Give me realistic benchmarks.

---

## 9. The Retention & Referral Machine

> My business [describe] just made a sale. Most businesses stop here. Don't let me. Build a complete post-purchase strategy that: makes the customer feel they made the best decision of their life, delivers unexpected value that creates loyalty, generates reviews and testimonials automatically, and turns satisfied customers into active referral sources. Give me the exact touchpoints, timing, and messages for each step.

---

## 10. The One-Page Marketing Strategy

> Using everything we've built together — my customer psychology, positioning, messaging, content plan, funnel, objection responses, email sequence, paid ads strategy, and retention system — compress it into a single one-page marketing strategy document for [business]. Format it as: Customer Avatar (3 lines), Core Message (1 line), Unique Position (2 lines), Top 3 Channels, 30-Day Action Plan (week by week), and the 3 KPIs that tell me my marketing is winning.
