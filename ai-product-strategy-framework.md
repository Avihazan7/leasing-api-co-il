## The 10 Pillars of AI Product Strategy

| # | Pillar | What it means |
|---|--------|---------------|
| 1 | **Problem First** | Solve real problems that matter. |
| 2 | **User & Workflow** | Fit AI naturally into user workflows. |
| 3 | **Data You Can Trust** | Use quality, accessible, and trustworthy data. |
| 4 | **Model & Approach** | Choose the right approach: RAG, fine-tuning, agents, or a combination. |
| 5 | **Security & Privacy** | Protect user data and stay compliant. |
| 6 | **Evaluation & Quality** | Measure what matters and iterate. |
| 7 | **Cost & Latency** | Deliver fast value at sustainable cost. |
| 8 | **Ownership & Governance** | Define ownership, roles, and responsible AI practices. |
| 9 | **Adoption & Change** | Drive adoption and help users succeed. |
| 10 | **Risk & Monitoring** | Monitor, mitigate, and continuously improve. |

## How to Choose the Right Approach

### A. Use RAG when…
- You need up-to-date answers from your own data.
- Knowledge changes often.
- You need fresh knowledge, source grounding, or access to changing/internal documents.

### B. Use Fine-Tuning when…
- You need consistent task behavior, style, format, or domain-specific patterns — and you have high-quality representative examples.
- The task is specific and predictable.

### C. Use Agents when…
- Tasks require multiple steps.
- You need tools/APIs.
- Decisions depend on context and planning.

---

## Common Mistakes to Avoid

- Solving a problem nobody feels.
- Using poor or outdated data.
- No evaluation or weak metrics.
- Ignoring cost & latency.
- Skipping governance & safety.

---

## Before You Ship — Checklist

- [ ] Problem validated with real users
- [ ] Data ready and accessible
- [ ] Approach chosen & tested
- [ ] Security, privacy, compliance & rollback reviewed
- [ ] Evaluation passed
- [ ] Monitored in production
